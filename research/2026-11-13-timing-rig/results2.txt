PicoClock research, 2026-11-13. Pico 2 W (RP2350, 150 MHz), MicroPython v1.29.0.
Pulse out on GP0 (or GP2..GP5), captured by a second PIO state machine through DMA,
so the measurement does not depend on the CPU. Capture resolution 2 cycles (13.3 ns).
